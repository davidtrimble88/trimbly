# Trimbly Home-Centered Refinement

## What will change

Refine the existing homepage presentation—not rebuild it—so it consistently feels like a personal home app. All current routes, controls, links, pricing, content, forms, authentication, and product behavior remain unchanged.

## Homepage refinements

1. **Make the opening feel personally owned**
   - Keep the exact headline and current daylight home photography.
   - Polish the overlaid diagnosis interface around “MY HOME,” with clearer cause, urgency, cost, next step, and a restrained turtle assistant cue.
   - Replace AI-forward supporting language with homeowner-first language while preserving the underlying capabilities.

2. **Strengthen the homeowner journey**
   - Keep the current sequence but clarify it as: understand the problem, know the cost, decide DIY or Pro, and get help when needed.
   - Make diagnosis, cost, coverage, maintenance, and the Home Binder the five dominant moments.

3. **Turn “Your Home” into the central app view**
   - Expand the current light home overview into a calm, realistic morning view with HVAC, gutters, weather, and water-heater status.
   - Add one concise “Trimbly says” recommendation using the existing turtle/house mark.
   - Keep the interface warm and domestic rather than analytical.

4. **Elevate the Digital Home Binder**
   - Make “THE MEMORY OF YOUR HOME” a strong visual anchor.
   - Present Water Heater, HVAC, Roof, documents, receipts, manuals, warranties, projects, and service history as an organized long-term record.

5. **Keep secondary capabilities available but subordinate**
   - Preserve the complete homeowner, provider, and mechanic feature catalog and its audience controls.
   - Reframe homeowner feature titles around problems and outcomes instead of repeatedly leading with “AI.”
   - Visually emphasize the five primary homeowner capabilities and quiet the remaining catalog so it does not compete with the home story.

6. **Keep Garage and pros in the right role**
   - Retain “YOUR HOME. YOUR GARAGE. ONE TRIMBLY.” and all Garage functionality, but use a lighter photo treatment so it does not overpower the home.
   - Keep providers, mechanics, bidding, messaging, rentals, and reviews intact while reinforcing that professional help follows understanding and cost guidance.

## Visual boundaries

- Continue the established warm white, white, navy, teal, green, blue, and restrained lime system with Outfit and Figtree.
- Use existing real residential photography and the existing turtle/house logo.
- Keep clean cards, moderate corners, subtle shadows, generous whitespace, and restrained motion.
- Do not add dark compositions, black overlays, purple gradients, abstract technology graphics, luxury-editorial styling, or an equal-weight feature wall.

## Verification

- Check desktop and phone layouts for clipping, overflow, readability, and visual hierarchy.
- Recheck the homepage audience tabs, feature links, hero actions, Home Binder, Garage, maintenance, professional pathways, and pricing controls.
- Confirm there are no new browser errors and no changes to routes or product behavior.
