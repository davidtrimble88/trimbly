# Trimbly Command-Center Positioning Refinement

## What will change

Refine the existing homepage hierarchy and copy—not its visual system—so Trimbly is immediately understood as the command center for the entire home. All routes, controls, pricing, forms, authentication, data, APIs, features, and product behavior remain unchanged.

## Homepage refinements

1. **Broaden the opening story**
   - Keep the exact “Tell it what's wrong. It tells you what to do.” headline and current daylight home photography.
   - Add “Your home has a lot to keep track of. Trimbly keeps track of it all.” as the larger positioning idea.
   - Explain that Trimbly helps homeowners maintain, understand, protect, organize, improve, and get help with their homes.
   - Keep the diagnosis interface as a strong example, not the definition of the whole product.

2. **Show breadth without a feature wall**
   - Add a concise “One app. Your entire home.” capability map centered on the home.
   - Group the existing capabilities by homeowner need: Fix It, Hire Help, Stay Ahead, Protect It, Remember It, and Understand It.
   - Keep Garage, shopping, rentals, and multiple homes visible as supporting extensions.

3. **Strengthen the home overview**
   - Retain the warm “Your Home” interface and turtle/house mark.
   - Reframe “Trimbly Says” around three timely home needs: HVAC filter, rain/gutters, and water-heater lifespan.
   - Keep the experience domestic and reassuring rather than analytical.

4. **Keep the detailed journey intact**
   - Preserve Home Binder, maintenance, coverage, Garage, multi-home, professional help, and the complete feature catalog.
   - Adjust section framing only where needed so diagnosis reads as one capability inside the larger platform.

## Visual boundaries

- Continue the established warm white, white, navy, teal, green, blue, and restrained lime system with Outfit and Figtree.
- Use existing real residential photography and the existing turtle/house logo.
- Keep the current clean cards, moderate corners, subtle shadows, whitespace, photography, and restrained motion.
- Do not add dark compositions, black overlays, purple gradients, abstract technology graphics, luxury-editorial styling, or an equal-weight feature wall.

## Verification

- Check desktop and phone layouts for clipping, overflow, readability, and first-screen clarity.
- Confirm the command-center idea, capability breadth, diagnosis example, and “Trimbly Says” overview all read clearly.
- Recheck hero actions, audience tabs, feature links, and pricing controls, with no browser errors or behavior changes.
